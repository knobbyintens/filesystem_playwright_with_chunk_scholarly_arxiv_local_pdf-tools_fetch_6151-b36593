Procurement intake

Submit the completed sourcing request for audit. The intake service records the request payload and forwards it to the buying desk.

Payload fields

  • request_id — the request identifier from the sourcing request note.
  • item_ids — the three shortlisted item identifiers, in shortlist order.
  • total_usd — the sum of the three buy-box unit prices, as a number.

The intake service accepts a JSON body either as a plain-text POST or as the payload form field. A submitted request is only recorded once the service receives the HTTP request.
