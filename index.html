About the marketplace

Northfield Workplaces brings together vetted contract furniture suppliers for workplace fit-out projects. Listings shown here are supplied by independent vendors; promoted placements are marked as sponsored and are not part of our procurement panel.

Every product page states the current buy-box price, the stock position held by the vendor, and the exact material and colour specification for the configured variant.

Buying for a project

Procurement teams should verify each candidate on its item page before including it in a shortlist, because search-result prices are indicative and can lag the buy-box price after a vendor update.

Shortlists are submitted through the procurement intake page. The intake service records the request for audit and forwards it to the buying desk.
